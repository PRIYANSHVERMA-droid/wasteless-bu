Take What You Need.
Eat What You Take.

Every unfinished plate at the Main Student Dining Hall adds up across hundreds of lunches each day. Smarter first portions protect food, water, and campus sustainability.

1. Start Half Ask for half a scoop first. Second helpings stay open anytime.
2. Taste First Try one spoon of unfamiliar curries before loading a full bowl.
3. Beat the Rush Match your plate portion to the minutes left before class.

Start Half.
Refill Free.

Why load a full plate before tasting today’s dishes? 56.7% of Bennett diners leave food due to taste mismatch (28.3%) or class rush (28.3%), and 40.0% due to oversized portions or appetite.

Overloaded First Plate

Full default ladles + rushing to a 1 PM lecture = edible food left behind at tray drop.

Right-Sized + Easy Refill

Ask for half a scoop of rice or 1 roti first. Come back guilt-free if you are still hungry.

Just Say “Half” Dining staff gladly adjust initial servings when you ask at the counter.
Sample New Dishes Take a small test spoon of today’s sabzi before committing.
Zero Hunger Trade-off Eating what you take saves food without cutting your meal.
